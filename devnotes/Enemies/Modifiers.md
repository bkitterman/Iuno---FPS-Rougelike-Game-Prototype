To be applied at random to enemies to change the combat loop and make it far more dangerous. 

# Effects
Applied to all.
- Backstabber - Double damage when shooting player in the back
- Rubber Banded - Teleports unpredictably
- Swift - Double move speed
- Perfected - All stats drastically increased beyond reason
- Dizzy - Really poor aim but extremely high damage
- Stealth - Invisible at long ranges
- Sniper - Much higher accuracy at reduced fire rate
- Assassin - Invisible at close range
- Plated - Extremely high health
- Body Armor - Immune to all but headshots
- Diseased - Damage causes bleeding
- Mirror Coated - Damage matching enemy type is reflect, but fragile
- Glass - Really high damage, really low health
- Barriered - Only damaged within a close range
- Greedy - Connects to enemies in the area and absorbs their stats
