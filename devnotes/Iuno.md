A roguelike, first person shooter. 

The game loop is simple: grab your gun, pick an ability (or two) and fight like hell through an endless wave of enemies across varying environments, tied together by a story I haven't decided on yet. 

The gun you pick has upgrades that modifies it directly. The abilities has these upgrade opportunities as well. There are general upgrades that affect all weapons, such as fire rate, damage, double shot, etc. as well as player upgrades, such as health, double jump, regen, and so on.

Health is a commodity to be used wisely.
